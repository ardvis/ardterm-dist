# Licensing and legal information

Open Ardterm → Legal & Privacy…, run `ardterm --licenses`, or read
`Ardterm.app/Contents/Resources/Legal/THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.txt` and
`Ardterm.app/Contents/Resources/Legal/SOFTWARE_RIGHTS.txt`.

These surfaces are included in releases built after the legal integration.
Read notices shipped with your exact release. Earlier archives are immutable;
this repository documentation does not add files to an installed older version.

Ardterm and its dependencies have separate rights and terms. Upstream
licenses apply to their respective components. No separate open-source license
for Ardvis-authored application code or this repository's distribution scripts
is recorded here. This document makes no new license grant and does not override
applicable distribution agreements, statutory rights, or third-party licenses.

Release archives must preserve original third-party copyright, license,
attribution, and disclaimer text. The bundled privacy summary describes software
behavior; service operators must supply their own approved privacy policy and
applicable terms. An SPDX inventory alone does not replace license notices.

Do not update published version assets in place. Prepare and review a new release
when notices or licensing information must change. The publisher must resolve
ownership, distribution terms, privacy, and export requirements before release.
